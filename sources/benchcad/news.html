2026-07-24

Claude Opus 5 on BenchCAD: 0.821 with tools, 0.366 without

Anthropic's Opus 5 system card reports it on BenchCAD's Vision2Code subset — 0.366 without tools, 0.821 with — and it upstreams two fixes to our reference (a camera-position typo and raw-shape grading).

BenchCAD Vision2Code ranking on 2026-07-24

The story isn't the single number — it's the slope. On BenchCAD, Claude's scores now scale steeply with test-time compute, especially once the model has tools to crop and verify its own renders. More effort buys markedly more accuracy.

The card also re-runs Opus 4.8 (0.277 / 0.521), Mythos 5 (0.378 / 0.678 on the subset, where its own card had 0.379 / 0.650) and Sonnet 5 (0.267 / 0.376) on the same harness as Opus 5.
