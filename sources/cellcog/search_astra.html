You pay for the work, not the hire. Hiring is free. Each time your employee works, they spend credits from your account: on Flash, built for speed, a working session typically runs under $5. On Max, our deepest reasoning, the same session runs up to about $25. From there the cost depends purely on how much work you assign. Start free, no credit card needed, with 200 credits.

A shift is one block of focused work, not an eight-hour day. Your employee wakes on a schedule or on a signal, works through what they planned, and ends with a report back to you. The next shift picks up where the last one left off, because they remember.

You set the permissions up front. You decide what they can do on their own, and anything dangerous waits for your approval. Every task and email they touch is visible to you on one board.
